# Trip legs

Issue #1563. Owner-approved 2026-09-29.

## The problem

A detected trip was one story. A trip that is a hike from village to village and then a few days
in a city was read as one story too, and favourites-first gave every slot to the starred hike. In the
reported film the city stay (four days, 101 eligible pictures, no stars) got 0 of 37 slots. The owner
asked for the city to be judged on its own: it may still get few slots, but as its own chapter.

## The signal: areas of stay

Legs come from geography. Place names were tried first and dropped: village-level geocoding flips
between neighbouring towns, so an island stay with day trips looked like a new place every day, and the
name signal did not even split the reported trip.

1. A day's position is the median of its pictures' coordinates.
2. Consecutive days form an **area** while each new day lies within 25 km of the area's median
   position. That is `CITY_SPREAD_KM`, the spread trip detection already calls one town.
3. An area of **3 days or more** is a **leg**. A shorter one (a travel day, a one-night stop) joins
   the leg before it, or the first leg when nothing comes before.
4. The trip splits only when it has two or more legs **and** consecutive legs lie more than 50 km
   apart (`AWAY_FROM_HOME_KM`).

A day without positions belongs to the leg of the day before it.

## Evidence (measured before building)

Three real trips from the launch matrix, profiled day by day:

| Trip | Shape | Result |
|---|---|---|
| Hike then city | 6 walking days moving 5–10 km a day, a transfer, 3–4 days in one city | two legs: 7 days (172 stars) and 3 days (0 stars), 185 km apart |
| Road trip | 13 days moving 15–125 km a day, one 2-night stop | one leg: no area holds 3 days |
| Island stay | 12 days on one island with day trips, a travel day at each end | one leg |

Radius sweep: at 15, 25 and 40 km the three answers do not change. At 10 km the island stay forms two
areas 2 km apart, and the 50 km guard keeps it whole; at 60 km the road trip forms one 4-day area, and
one leg alone never splits a trip.

Accepted consequence: three days at the beach and then three in the mountains more than 50 km away are
two legs. That is two chapters. A trip shorter than 6 days can never split.

## Where it applies

- `analysis/trip_legs.py`: `legs_of_days(points_by_day)` returns the leg of each day.
- The rules reader (`RuleStructureReader._runs`): a stretch away from home stays whole, except that it
  is cut at leg boundaries. This is the no-model path, and the path the reported film took.
- The model path (`editorial_story_trips`): a trip inside a film becomes one story per leg
  (`TripStories`), each titled after the stop it stayed at longest, each with its own reserve from its
  own days. A trip film gets `LegStories`, which splits any reader story that spans two legs.

The no-indicator rules then apply per leg, as they do to any story.

## Tests

`tests/test_trip_legs.py` covers the four approved cases (hike then city splits, a road trip does not,
one stay with day trips does not, two stays under 50 km apart do not), travel days and days without
positions, and the radius sweep. `tests/test_editorial_rule_trip_legs.py` covers the rules reader, and
`tests/test_editorial_story_trip_legs.py` covers both model-path folds.
