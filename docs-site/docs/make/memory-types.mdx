# Choose a film

Pick the subject in **Memory type**. Start with **Monthly Highlights** if you are trying the app. Every type works on the default Basic setup; some need a little more context from your library.

| Make a film of | Choose | Before you start |
|---|---|---|
| A month | Monthly Highlights | Pick a year and month |
| A whole year | Year in Review | Pick a year |
| Summer or another season | Season | Pick a season and year |
| One person | Person Spotlight | Name their face in Immich |
| Several people | Multi-person | Name the faces; choose all together or any of them |
| This date across the years | On This Day | Have pictures from several years |
| Christmas or another recurring occasion | Holiday | Pick a holiday; it covers every year you photographed it |
| A holiday away | Trip | Set home coordinates and have GPS-tagged pictures |
| Your own collection | Album | Create the album in Immich |
| One memorable day | Special Day | Pick the day, or use a discovered suggestion |

## What each one looks like

These films were made from the [demo library](../welcome/demo-library.md): one household's July, ten days at home and a road trip to the North Cape, all real photographs under CC0 or CC BY. Each one is a single real run on the Full tier with a local model, kept as it came out. The bars show what the run read and what it kept; the lines under them say why the rest stayed out.

The library is made of strangers' stock photos and one photographer's holiday, so the stories are thinner than in a real family's library. Treat the films as what the app does with what it's given, not as the best it can do.

## How long a film runs

Leave the length empty and the app fits it to the material. A busy month can make about a minute; a thin month may be much shorter. A year has a larger budget, not twelve glued-together monthly films.

For a 30-second portrait film, enter **0.5** in **Length in minutes (fitted to the pictures when empty)**, then choose **Portrait** under **Render > Orientation**. On the CLI, use `--short-form 30 --orientation portrait`. It is a target, and the app will not pad a period just to reach it. [Exact length rules](../reference/film-types.mdx#how-long-a-film-runs) are available when you need them.

## People and birthdays

For one person, choose their name and the period. For several, choose whether pictures need **all of them together** or **any of them**. A picture only counts when Immich recognised that person on the picture itself: a back-of-head shot or a photo where they're just out of frame drops out, even if they were there.

Set birth dates in Immich to use birthday windows. Confirm [home and relationships](../get-started/who-is-who.md) so the film knows who matters. Advanced grouped conditions, all-years windows and birthday compilations are in the [film type reference](../reference/film-types.mdx#filtering-by-people).

## Trip

Set [your home base](../get-started/who-is-who.md) first. Pick **Trip** and a year, then choose one of the detected trips. A trip crossing New Year stays together.

No trip found: check that your pictures have GPS and that home is configured. Detection uses distance, nights away and gaps in activity; [the trip reference](../reference/film-types.mdx#trip) gives the defaults and adjustments.

Maps are optional and use outside satellite tiles. A trip works with ordinary titles when maps are off. [Enable maps](../reference/output-rendering.md#the-map-fly-over).

## Album

Use an Immich album when you already know what belongs together. Choose **Album** and pick it; the app still makes an edit from the collection. On the CLI, a [reader](../better/reader.md) can use a declared subject for collections such as cars or baking: `--from-album "Cars" --subject "cars"`. The web album chooser has no subject field.

## Holiday

Pick the holiday. The film reads every year your library has around the day, up to 30 years back, and **Years to span** (`--years-back` on the CLI) narrows it to the most recent ones. The day itself leads each year; the two days either side only join when the day holds fewer than 5 pictures. Every year gets an even share, so one busy Christmas can't take over twenty. The title is the holiday's name and the years the cut shows: "Christmas · 2019–2025". [Holiday rules and calendars](../reference/film-types.mdx#holiday).

## Special day

Choose a day directly, or let [special-day discovery](./cli/discover-days.md) build a catalogue of occasions. A catalogue title can be edited. Discovery is optional; you can film a day that is not in it.

The [complete reference](../reference/film-types.mdx) covers holiday calendars, people expressions, account lookup, birthdays and budgets. [Automatic films](./automate.md) can choose a subject for you.
