# Known limitations for now

The editor was tested on households with kids, pets, and light use. This page says where the films hold up and where they don't yet.

## Family libraries: Basic and Full both work

Months, trips, years and person films come out with the right people, in the order things happened, and almost always without screenshots or receipts. Basic got there on its own.

A story's share of the film is capped at the moments it can show distinctly, and the rest of the budget goes to stories with unfunded moments elsewhere, so a film only comes out short when the library genuinely has no more distinct material to give it.

Full still does better on the same films: captions tell moments apart before the slots are handed out, so fewer picks get thrown away as copies in the first place.

## Pets, hobbies and light users: Basic gives something, but not their subject

Months and trips work for every household: a pet-focused one, a hobbyist one, a light user's trip all come out full length and sensible.

What doesn't work yet is anything about what the household actually cares about:

- **A quiet household keeps its ordinary weeks.** Basic gives a slot to days with an occasion (a trip, a favourite, a video, close family); a cat asleep on the sofa is none of those. When a household's at-home weeks mostly carry no occasion indicator at all, Basic funds each of them with its own sharpest, best-exposed clean picture instead of letting a single outing take every leftover slot. It still can't tell Basic that the subject is the cat: a week that holds both a cat and a dozen pictures of somewhere else just picks whichever reads as the better picture.
- **A sparse library keeps its quiet weeks too.** For someone who takes a few snapshots a month, those quiet weeks ARE the year, and the same funding-by-best-picture rule covers them.
- **A face Immich rarely sees makes a thin person film.** A rarely-recognised face makes for very few usable shots. The editor only knows who is in a picture through Immich's face recognition.
- **A couple film needs both faces in one picture.** One of you usually holds the camera, so a year may hold only a handful of pictures of the two of you together.

Full does not fix the subject problem on its own: it refines the shortlist Basic draws, so a household still gets no film about the pet or the hobby specifically. Basic has no way to ask for the pet or the hobby either.

## Asking in your own words is the way in, on Full

[Free text](../make/free-text.md) is what gets these households their film. A request like "our
cats over the years" or "my knitting projects" finds every shot on that subject across years, not
just one period. It prepares whatever its own period needs on its own, with a warning first if
that takes a while, and a second request over the same period skips straight to a fresh check.

See [measured setups](../better/measured.md) for how long preparation actually takes.

Smaller open problems live in [the issue tracker](https://github.com/sam-dumont/immich-memories/issues).
