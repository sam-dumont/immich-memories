# Immich Memories

Immich Memories turns the photos and videos in your Immich library into short films with titles and music. Pick a month, a year, a trip or a person, review the cut it proposes, then render.

A road trip to Nordkapp, 44 seconds, made from [credited Wikimedia Commons photographs](https://github.com/sam-dumont/immich-memories/blob/main/tests/e2e/fixtures/library/CREDITS.md) (CC0 or CC BY). The map is optional.

## Make your first film {#start-with-one-month}

You need an existing [Immich](https://immich.app) library, an API key and a machine with Docker. A plain NAS makes the whole film; a GPU is optional.

Follow [Quick start](../get-started/quick-start.md): choose Basic if you are unsure, download its two setup files, connect Immich and open the app. The same page has the GPU and Full paths. Then [make your first film](../get-started/first-film.mdx) from an album of 20–50 pictures, and review every shot before rendering.

## How it chooses

It groups your library into stories, gives busy days and the people who matter more room, and plays the shots in the order they happened. Favourites help. A video often carries a moment better than a still. A thin period makes a shorter film instead of padding it.

You can remove a shot, swap it, trim a video or add a missing picture. [How it chooses](../how-it-chooses/overview.md) explains the general idea; [improve a cut](../how-it-chooses/overrule-it.md) shows where your choices win.

## Your library stays yours

The default install reads your pictures locally and talks only to your Immich server. It has no telemetry, checks for updates only when you ask, and uploads a finished film only when you tell it to. Downloading the app and its pinned models needs internet access; optional maps, readers and remote services have their own [privacy settings](../run/privacy.md).
