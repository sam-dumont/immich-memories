# Your first film

Use an Immich album with **20–50 photos and short videos**, and make a **30-second film**.
A small album keeps the first preparation manageable. These steps work on Basic, GPU and Full.

## Before you start {#prepare-and-check-first}

Finish your [installation](./quick-start.md), including `models fetch` and `preflight` for the
tier you chose. Open the app in your browser. There is no need to prepare your whole library
or configure home and people before an album film.

## 1. Choose an album {#pick-the-trial-album}

In Immich, use an existing small album or create one from 20–50 photos and videos.
In Immich Memories:

1. Open **Memory** and choose **Album** under **Memory type**.
2. Select your album. Its picture count appears in the dropdown.
3. Open **Length and pictures** and set **Length in minutes** to **0.5**.

Keep your tier's configured services. Bundled music is ready to use; leave upload off for
this first film so you can download it locally.

## 2. Make the cut {#cut}

Press **Cut**. The app prepares the pictures and chooses the shots. The first cut takes longer;
compatible preparation is reused for later films. A shorter output does not reduce the input
pool, which is why this first film uses a small album.

When the cut finishes, its run opens. You can also find it under **Runs**.

## 3. Review the shots {#read-the-cut}

The contact sheet shows the shots in playback order. Open one to see it larger, watch its video
interval and read why it was selected. Fewer shots than source pictures is normal: bursts and
similar pictures often become one shot.

### Make a change {#fix-it}

Use **Remove from this cut**, **Start here** / **End here** to trim, or **Use this picture instead**
to swap a shot. Press **Save revision**. To add a missing picture, open **Pool**, select it and
press **Preview with these choices**.

<ThemedScreenshot name="first-film-edit" alt="Editing a shot and viewing its alternatives" />

[Editing a cut](../how-it-chooses/overrule-it.md) covers the other controls, including choices that
apply to future films.

## 4. Render and watch {#render-and-watch}

Below the contact sheet, find **Render**. Choose your cut or saved revision under **What to render**,
then press **Render**. Leave **Upload the film to Immich** off.

When the film is ready, the run shows a player and **Download film** underneath it.
Watch it with sound, then download the MP4.

<ThemedScreenshot name="memory-export" alt="The finished film in the player" />

Films stay in your configured output storage: `./output` with the release Compose file,
a Docker volume with the stack-editor setup, or `~/Videos/Memories` on a native install.
A successful upload to Immich removes the local copy; leave upload off to keep it here.

## While it works {#progress-and-recovery}

The app reports preparation, cut and render progress. Reloading the browser does not cancel
work. Return to **Memory** for an active cut or **Runs** for a saved cut or render.

Use **Cancel** to stop a job. For a failure or interrupted run, see
[film progress and recovery](../run/film-progress.md); compatible prepared facts remain cached.

## Your next film {#next}

Choose **Monthly Highlights**, a year and a month. A larger period needs more preparation.
Set [home and people](./who-is-who.md) for trips and family films, or follow
[After install](./after-install.md) for backups and automatic films.
