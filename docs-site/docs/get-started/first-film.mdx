# Your first film

Start with a month that has a few busy days, some favourites and a few videos. It keeps the first cut manageable and gives you something worth watching.

This assumes [installation](./quick-start.md) is done and `models fetch` has run.

## Pick a month

Open [http://localhost:8080](http://localhost:8080). Under **Memory type**, pick **Monthly Highlights**, a year and a month. Leave the length empty: the app fits it to the material.

<ThemedScreenshot name="first-film-brief" alt="The brief with Monthly Highlights selected" />

If Immich does not connect, check the URL and API key in `.env`, then recreate the container with `docker compose up -d`. Values set in the environment cannot be changed in **Settings**.

## Cut

Press **Cut**. The app prepares the pictures and builds the edit. The first cut takes longer; compatible preparation results are reused on later cuts. You can reload the page while it works.

## Read the cut

The cut opens its run page under **Runs**. The contact sheet shows the shots in playback order. Open one to see it larger, watch its video interval and read why it stayed. **Stories** groups the same shots by story.

Favourites help a moment earn its place. Videos often carry it better than a still. Quiet weeks can be empty: the film goes short instead of padding. [How it chooses](../how-it-chooses/overview.md) explains this without requiring you to configure it.

## Fix it

For a wrong shot, open its inspector. **Remove from this cut**, trim with **Start here** and **End here**, or choose **Use this picture instead**. Press **Save revision**.

For a missing shot, open **Pool**, tick the picture and press **Preview with these choices**. These edits make a revision of this cut; they do not rerun selection.

<ThemedScreenshot name="first-film-edit" alt="Editing a shot and viewing its alternatives" />

**Never use** is different: it excludes the picture from future films. Undo that decision before trying to add it again. [Improve a cut](../how-it-chooses/overrule-it.md) covers lasting changes.

## Render and watch

Under **Render**, pick the cut or your revision in **What to render**, then press **Render**. The defaults are fine for a first film.

<ThemedScreenshot name="memory-export" alt="The finished film in the player" />

On Docker, local films land in `./output`. If you select **Upload the film to Immich**, a successful upload removes the local copy. This also happens for CLI and automatic uploads. The run remains in **Runs**, with its link to Immich. Leave upload off when you want the file locally.

## Next

Set [home and people](./who-is-who.md), then choose [another kind of film](../make/memory-types.mdx). Titles, format and music can wait until you like the cut.

## The same on the CLI

```bash
immich-memories generate --memory-type monthly_highlights --year 2025 --month 6
```

In Docker, prefix it with `docker compose exec immich-memories`. [CLI recipes](../make/cli/generate.md) include reviewing a saved cut before rendering.
